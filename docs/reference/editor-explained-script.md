# CashCow Studio, the editor explained: narration script

Video: `cashcow-editor-explained-2026-10-11.mp4` in this folder (3 minutes 43 seconds, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, 69 MB). Made on 2026-10-11 for the editing team.

What it shows:
- **Planned (M5 mockups).** Most of the video walks through the design for the new Edit room and Storyboard v2. These screens are drawn mockups from `docs/design` (`mockup-editor.html`, `mockup-storyboard.html`), not the working app yet. The top bar of each part says "PLANNED · M5 MOCKUP".
- **Built today.** Parts 13 and 20 to 22 show what editors can use now: the Edit review, the transition picker, the storyboard card fields and the real transition previews. The top bar says "BUILT TODAY". Parts 19 and 23 sum up the plan and say "THE PLAN".
- The facts come from `docs/design/EDITOR-DESIGN.md` (sections 1, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 12) and `docs/design/README.md`.

Narrator: the Windows Zira voice at normal speed. Each part shows one screen with a slow zoom or pan, its name in a bar at the top, and an amber outline on the thing being described. Parts are joined by half-second crossfades.
